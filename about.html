About Me

Civil engineer and construction manager with more than 10 years of experience delivering mining infrastructure, renewable energy, industrial construction and heavy civil works in Türkiye and Burkina Faso.

Engineering Leadership

Building clarity across complex projects

My career has focused on turning technically demanding projects into coordinated, practical and safely delivered construction outcomes. I have managed multidisciplinary teams, contractors, procurement, quality, programme and stakeholder interfaces across projects in Türkiye and Burkina Faso.

My experience includes tailings storage facilities, mine infrastructure, wind power projects, industrial construction and renovation works. I work closely with designers and Engineers of Record while maintaining clear responsibility for construction planning, field execution, commercial coordination and delivery performance.
