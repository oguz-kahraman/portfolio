About Me

I am a Civil Engineer and Construction Manager with over fourteen years of professional experience delivering complex infrastructure, mining, renewable energy and industrial construction projects across Türkiye and Burkina Faso.

Throughout my career, I have managed multidisciplinary construction teams, coordinated contractors, consultants and clients, and delivered technically demanding projects where safety, quality, programme performance and commercial objectives were equally critical.

My experience combines practical construction management with sound engineering judgement. I have consistently worked at the interface between engineering, construction, commercial management and stakeholder coordination, enabling projects to progress efficiently while maintaining alignment with engineering standards, contractual requirements and project objectives.

A defining stage of my career was serving as Construction Manager at the Perkoa Zinc Mine in Burkina Faso, where I coordinated the delivery of critical Tailings Storage Facility infrastructure. Working closely with the client, contractors and the Engineer of Record, I was responsible for construction planning, procurement coordination, contractor management, quality assurance, risk management and project execution. This work supported the timely delivery of infrastructure required for the continuation of mining operations.

I also managed the construction of utility-scale wind energy projects in Türkiye, including the installation of seven Nordex N131 wind turbines. These projects required complex logistics planning, multidisciplinary coordination and practical engineering solutions for transporting oversized wind turbine blades through mountainous terrain. The transportation solution later received national media recognition for its engineering application.

In addition to major industrial and infrastructure projects, my experience includes commercial, institutional and residential construction, providing me with a broad understanding of construction management across different project types and delivery environments.

I remain committed to continuous professional development through internationally recognised qualifications and professional memberships, including Project Management Professional (PMP®), Graduate Membership of the Institution of Civil Engineers (GMICE), and Fellowship of the Society of Construction Law. I also continue to develop my knowledge in infrastructure, project delivery and asset management.

I am currently based in the United States, where I continue my professional development while pursuing further international engineering recognition and preparing for the next stage of my career.
